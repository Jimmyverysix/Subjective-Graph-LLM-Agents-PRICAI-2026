Social and Mental Health Survey Questionnaire

Dear Students: Hello! To better support the mental health and interpersonal development of the students, the school will organize a survey. This survey strictly follows the principle of voluntariness and will be conducted with the students' consent.

This questionnaire will collect basic information about you, your social situation in the class, and your views on the relationships among classmates. Some parts of the questionnaire require real-name filling. We will use and protect the data in strict accordance with relevant laws and regulations, and will strictly protect your personal information. All data will only be used for the writing of research papers or reports, and will not be publicly displayed or analyzed individually. All presented results are statistical summaries and will not include your name, student number, or any other personal information.

We sincerely thank you for your support and cooperation! If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us at any time.
Answering on this test paper is invalid. Please answer on the corresponding area of the answer sheet!
This test consists of 3 parts, with a total of 15 questions. It is estimated to take 10 minutes.
